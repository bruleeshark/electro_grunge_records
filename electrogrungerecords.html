Revenue Flow

how real-world income enters the protocol

01 — Revenue In

Streaming royalties, merch, live events & licensing land in the Coinbase Business Account as fiat. Legacy DSP systems mean 3–6 month lag. Protocol targets yearly LP additions.

02 — USDC On-Chain

Fiat converts to USDC via Coinbase. Minted or bought on-chain — whichever is cheaper. Threshold: roughly >$5k favors minting via crvUSD vault. Below that, swap on Curve or 1inch.

03 — scrvUSD

USDC converts to scrvUSD — Curve's yield-bearing stablecoin. Earns from crvUSD borrowing activity continuously. Counter-cyclical: yield rises when crvUSD dips below peg.

04 — LP Addition

scrvUSD + 💾 enter the Curve pool. LP tokens minted. Sent to EGR Treasury multisig. Pool deepens. Slippage drops. Fee revenue rises. The treasury compounds.
